== Problem

The biggest blocker to AI automation of companies is no longer model
capability — models have crossed the necessary threshold. The blocker is
*domain knowledge*.

Every company has critical know-how scattered everywhere:

* in people’s heads
* in old email accounts
* in Slack threads
* in support tickets
* in databases, dashboards, and runbooks
* in past decisions buried inside meeting recordings

The company works because humans vaguely remember where that knowledge
lives and how to apply it. AI agents cannot operate this way. For every
company to run on AI automation, we need a new primitive: *a company
brain*.

=== What the company brain is

A system that:

[arabic]
. Pulls knowledge out of all the fragmented sources where it lives
today.
. Structures it into a legible, navigable form.
. Keeps it current as the company changes.
. Exposes it in a form AI systems can use to do real work, safely and
consistently.

It is not a company-wide search. It is not a chatbot over documents. It
is a *living map of how a company actually works* — how refunds get
handled, how pricing exceptions are decided, how engineers respond to
incidents, how a deal moves from qualified to closed.

=== Why this is the missing layer

Without a company brain, every AI deployment inside a company has to
re-derive the same context from scratch, gets it wrong in inconsistent
ways, and cannot be trusted with autonomous action. With one, AI systems
have a stable, auditable source of the operational truth they need.

Every company in the world will need one.
