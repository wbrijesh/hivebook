== Audience

Trenches is sold business-to-business. Individuals are not the buyer.

=== Buyer profile

The buyer is a company that:

* has fragmented operational knowledge across multiple SaaS tools and
human heads
* already runs (or wants to run) AI agents that need that knowledge
* has the security, compliance, and procurement posture of a real
business — i.e., enterprise sales motion, not credit-card self-serve

=== Open question: mid-market vs. true enterprise for the first 18 months

These are different products in practice. The decision changes storage
architecture, deployment model, and sales motion.

[width="100%",cols="21%,39%,40%",options="header",]
|===
|Dimension |Mid-market (200–2k employees) |True enterprise (5k{plus})
|Deployment |SaaS only |SaaS, VPC, or on-prem inference
|Storage |Single multi-tenant region |Customer-pinned region, BYO storage
|Auth |SSO (SAML/OIDC) {plus} SCIM |Plus fine-grained policy engine, CMK
|Sales cycle |Weeks |Quarters
|Procurement bar |Security questionnaire |Pen-test reports, SOC2/ISO, DPIA, MSAs
|Per-customer ARR |$20k–150k |$250k{plus}
|===

A decision here is needed before the architecture and roadmap docs can
be made concrete. Until then, the architecture is designed to *leave
the door open to true enterprise* (tenant isolation, ACL capture,
residency tagging, BYOK-ready encryption are day-zero) without
requiring the full enterprise stack on day one.
