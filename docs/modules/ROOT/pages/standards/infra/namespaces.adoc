= Infra · Namespaces
:status: living
:last-reviewed: 2026-06-06

One namespace per platform concern. These are Kubernetes _deployment_ namespaces
— unrelated to per-customer `tenant_id`
(xref:ROOT:architecture/tenancy-and-acl.adoc[tenancy]).

[width="100%",cols="22%,78%",options="header"]
|===
|Namespace |Holds

|`traefik` |Ingress controller — TLS termination, routing
|`cert-manager` |Cert issuance from the mkcert CA
|`zitadel` |Identity: ZITADEL + its own Postgres
|`observability` |VictoriaMetrics, VictoriaLogs, Vector, Grafana
|`hivebook` |All app workloads — api, web, docs, app Postgres
|===

== Rules

. *New app workloads go in `hivebook`* — don't spin a namespace per service.
. *Platform components get their own namespace* when they own distinct
operational state (their own DB, operator, or lifecycle).
. *Each namespace that terminates TLS has its own wildcard `Certificate`*
producing `hivebook-wildcard-tls` — Traefik resolves `tls.secretName` in the
route's own namespace.
. *Manifests mirror the component* under `infra/<component>/` (`infra/api/`,
`infra/zitadel/`…), applied by the matching `just` recipe.
. *`infra/namespaces.yaml` is the source of truth* for which namespaces exist.
