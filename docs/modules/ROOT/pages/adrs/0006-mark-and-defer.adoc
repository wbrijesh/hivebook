== Context

Structural changes happen continuously and must not block the requester
or cost a rebuild each. Always-stale derived state is also unacceptable.

== Decision

*Mark-and-defer.* Every structural change synchronously marks affected
summaries and their ancestors stale (O(1); at most 4 entries), then
returns. Rebuilds happen lazily — on read pressure or in the
maintenance loop — and are idempotent, keyed by the evidence content
hash.

== Alternatives considered

* *Eager rebuild* — unaffordable, slow admin operations, thundering
cascades.
* *No staleness signal* — readers cannot tell what to trust.
* *Periodic full rebuild* — wasteful and slow to freshen.

== Consequences

Structural changes are fast and synchronous; staleness is visible to
readers; cost concentrates in the off-peak, batched maintenance loop.
Worst-case staleness is bounded by `MAX_STALE_AGE`, a tunable default
(xref:ROOT:adrs/0011-constants-first-tunables.adoc[ADR-0011]).
