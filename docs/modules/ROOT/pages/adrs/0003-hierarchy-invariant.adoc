== Context

Summaries derive from evidence and from each other. Where citations
attach determines the shape of the rebuild graph.

== Decision

*Citations live only at the sub-topic level.* Sub-topic summaries cite
raw artifacts; topic summaries cite their child sub-topic summaries;
chapter summaries cite their child topic summaries. This is the
*hierarchy invariant*.

== Alternatives considered

* *Citations at every level pointing at raw artifacts* — explodes the
rebuild graph and duplicates evidence.
* *No structured citations* — breaks trust and stale-detection.

== Consequences

Fixed-depth-3 rebuild graph (with xref:ROOT:adrs/0002-three-level-hierarchy.adoc[ADR-0002]). Every claim traces to
verbatim evidence in a bounded number of hops, and stale-detection is
local. Cost: a chapter-level claim is two hops from its evidence.
