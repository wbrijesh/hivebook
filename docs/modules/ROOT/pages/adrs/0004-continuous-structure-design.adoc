== Context

Each customer's knowledge has a different shape, and the book structure
must fit it. The structure can be designed up front or grown from data.

== Decision

No per-customer bootstrap. Every customer starts from a thin generic
chapter skeleton (5-8 broad chapters); topics and sub-topics begin
empty. The filing pipeline designs and redesigns structure continuously
as content arrives.

== Alternatives considered

* *Upfront per-customer ontology* — slow and expensive, and wrong
before any ingestion data exists.
* *Per-vertical templates* — real organizations vary too much;
templates either shoehorn content or proliferate.

== Consequences

Onboarding is instant and structure emerges from real data. Early
batches produce volatile structure that the maintenance loop
consolidates over the first weeks.
