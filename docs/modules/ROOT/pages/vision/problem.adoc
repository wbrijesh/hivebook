= Problem
:status: stable
:last-reviewed: 2026-06-06

The blocker to AI automation of companies is no longer model
capability — it is *domain knowledge*. Models have crossed the
necessary threshold; the operational know-how they need has not been
made available to them.

Every company's critical know-how is scattered: in people's heads, old
email, Slack threads, support tickets, databases, dashboards, runbooks,
and meeting recordings. Companies work because humans vaguely remember
where it lives and how to apply it. AI agents cannot operate that way.

== The missing primitive: a company brain

A company brain is a system that:

[arabic]
. Pulls knowledge out of the fragmented sources where it lives today.
. Structures it into a legible, navigable form.
. Keeps it current as the company changes.
. Exposes it in a form AI systems can use to do real work, safely and
consistently.

It is not company-wide search and not a chatbot over documents. It is a
*living map of how a company actually works* — how refunds get handled,
how pricing exceptions get decided, how engineers respond to incidents,
how a deal moves from qualified to closed.

== Why it is the missing layer

Without a company brain, every AI deployment re-derives the same context
from scratch, gets it wrong inconsistently, and cannot be trusted with
autonomous action. With one, AI systems have a stable, auditable source
of operational truth. Every company will need one.
